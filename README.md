## What is Charactry?

Charactry is a dedicated space for original-character (OC) creators. Instead of scattering your cast across notes apps, Google Docs, and a dozen folders, you get one tidy place for **profiles, art, universes, relationships, lore, and stories**, all made to be looked at, not just filed away.

It's **local-first**: your characters live on your device, not on someone else's server. No account needed to use it.

You can use it two ways, and they share the same interface and the same license:

| | **Windows app** | **Web app** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Download & install (.exe) | Just open the link, nothing to install |
| Devices | Windows | Phone, tablet, or computer: Mac, Linux, iOS, Android too |
| Your data | Stored on your computer | Stored in your browser, on your device |
| Offline | Yes, fully | Data lives on your device; installable to your home screen |
| Plugins | Drop-in folders | Install straight from a `.zip` or `.rar` |

> **Web app:** <https://enjouakuma.github.io/charactry/>

## ✨ Features (free)

The core app is completely free. No paywalled basics.

### Characters
- **Full character profiles**: names, nicknames, pronouns, age, height, species, alignment, appearance, traits, likes/dislikes, strengths/weaknesses, backstory, abilities, trivia, quotes, and more.
- **Avatars & banners** with a built-in cropper, an image gallery per character, and **custom tabs/fields** for anything your world needs (powers, school records, theme songs, whatever).
- **Rarity system**: tag OCs as Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic, each with its own animated border and particle effects. Because some characters just deserve to glow.
- **Folders & subfolders**, **favorites**, **tags**, and instant **search** across your whole cast.
- **Clone**, **export/import** a single character, and downloadable **character sheets**.

### Universes & AUs
- Organize characters across **multiple universes and alternate AUs**, each with its own color, description, timeline, and (optional) theme.
- **Per-universe variants**: the same OC reimagined in a different setting, with its own separate data and art.
- Switch worlds from the top bar in one click.

### Relationships
- Map who knows who: family, friends, lovers, rivals, enemies, mentors, and 100+ built-in relationship types, or type your own.
- Color-code relationship types and view every connection on a character's profile.

### Worldbuilding
- **Worlds, Locations, Factions, Species, Magic Systems, and a Timeline**, all linkable to your characters.
- **Lorebooks**: keyword-based world knowledge you write once and reuse.
- **Lore Notes** with categories and pinning, and a **Stories** editor with chapters, Markdown, live preview, and `@Character` mentions.

### Creative tools
- **OC Generator** and **AU Generator** for when inspiration takes a nap.
- **Randomizer**, **Writing Prompts**, **Activity tracker**, and a **Command palette** with keyboard shortcuts.

### Make it yours
- Multiple base themes, custom accent colors and gradients, and fine-grained UI color overrides.
- **10 languages**: English, 日本語, 한국어, 中文, 粵語 (Cantonese), Español, Français, Português (BR), Русский, Deutsch.

## 🌐 Using the web version

- **Your data stays with you.** Everything is saved in your browser on your device. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, it's all still there. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
- **Back it up.** Clearing your browser's site data (or using a private window) erases it, so use **Settings → Download full backup** every now and then, and to move to another device.
- **Install it like an app.** On a phone or tablet, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen".
- **AI Studio on the web (PLUS):** connect your own [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) account (free models available), any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local Ollama, under **AI Studio → Setup**. Your key stays in your browser.
- **Plugins:** the eight bundled plugins are included. To add more, use **Settings → Install plugin** and pick a `.zip` or `.rar` file.

## 🔌 Plugins & community

Know some HTML and JavaScript? You can build plugins that slot right into Charactry's sidebar and share them.

- Free sharing is always welcome. **Selling plugins requires permission** from the developer.
- Be upfront about any sensitive content, no malicious code, credit yourself, and don't bypass or tamper with PLUS verification.
- **Only install plugins from people you trust**, since plugins run inside Charactry.

Full guide: see the [documentation](https://enjouakuma.neocities.org/charactry_docs.html).
